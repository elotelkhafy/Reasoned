# Reasoned - Build Your Essay

### Read deeply. Think clearly. Respond thoughtfully.

Afterword is an intellectual practice platform designed to help people become better readers, thinkers, writers, and conversationalists.

Instead of simply consuming literature and ideas, users actively engage with them.

**Read → Think → Write → Get Challenged → Revise**

## The MVP

Users:

1. Read a curated piece of literature or intellectual writing.
2. Answer a challenging question about the text.
3. Submit a 300–500 word response.
4. Review another reader's response.
5. Receive Socratic-style questions designed to challenge their reasoning.
6. Revise their original response.
7. Track how their thinking and writing develop over time.

## Why?

Most reading platforms measure what you read.

Most writing tools focus on correcting what you wrote.

Afterword focuses on the space between the two:

> **What did you understand, what do you think, and can you defend it?**
